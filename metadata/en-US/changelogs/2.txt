Fixes the power and net pages on phones with a work profile. The power page shows much more: charger watts, average current, cell details and, with Shizuku, everything since the last charge (drain with the screen on and off, doze, learned capacity, time left, use by part of the phone). Scan puts each name on its own line. Shizuku calls are time-limited and retried; one tap can switch on usage and notification access through it.
